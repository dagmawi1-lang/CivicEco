# CivicEco Privacy Notes

CivicEco is designed to collect only information needed for its community features.

Potentially stored data includes:
- account name and email
- environmental reports
- report locations supplied by the user
- evidence attached to reports
- confirmations, follows, comments, notifications
- response-plan participation and measurements

CivicEco should not collect a user's precise location unless the user explicitly chooses a location-based feature.

For a public deployment, add:
1. a visible privacy policy,
2. account/data deletion,
3. retention limits,
4. clear photo/evidence handling rules,
5. secure production storage,
6. a process for reporting inappropriate content.

Environmental intelligence is decision support. It should not be presented as proof of causation or as an official government determination.
